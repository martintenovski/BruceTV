Glass look
- Glassy and Frosted glass are redone: the dock, the buttons and the Control Center now blur the wallpaper behind them, with a soft rim and highlight
- The Control Center is darker in these styles, so it reads on any wallpaper
- In light mode the wallpaper is shown as it is, with nothing darkening it

Dark mode
- Reworked: grey glass buttons, a lighter dock, and app icons shaded darker instead of see-through
- A lighter dark layer over the wallpaper

Dock layout
- New "Fill the dock" switch (Icons): with 3 or more apps, the tiles share the whole dock
- New switch to hide the Recent button on the dock
- Down on an app opens All apps, and Up from the top row of All apps goes back Home
- Equal spacing on the left and right of the dock, and the focused app now sits above its neighbours
- New "Clock widget" setting: Default, or Minimal (a small clock and the weather at the top left)
- The clock is bold, and stays readable on bright wallpapers

Settings
- The Control Center is now a list of sections (Quick actions, Network and sound, Display and wallpaper, Home screen, Game Mode, System); open one to see its settings
- New "Show hints" switch (Icons) to hide tips like "Hold OK on an app for options"

Fixes
- The update window no longer runs off small screens: the notes scroll and the buttons always fit
- Moving up and down in All apps now plays the same sound as left and right
